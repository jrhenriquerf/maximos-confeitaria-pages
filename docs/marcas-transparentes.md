# Logos com transparência

As versões originais foram preservadas. A remoção do fundo foi realizada com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, no modo de edição com fundo transparente. Nenhuma fotografia de produto foi gerada ou alterada pela ferramenta.

Arquivos finais: `public/images/logo-transparent.png` e `public/images/mini-transparent.png`. A interface utiliza as respectivas versões WebP otimizadas, preservando o canal alfa; o favicon também possui transparência.

## Prompts utilizados

Logo principal:

> Edit target: the attached existing Maximos Confeitaria logo. Background extraction ONLY: remove the entire beige/cream background to actual alpha transparency, including inside counters of letters and inside the strawberry outline. Preserve EXACT existing lettering MAXIMOS (the strawberry replaces O), CONFEITARIA, letter spacing, fine serif shapes, thin horizontal lines, terracotta color and relative proportions. Do not redesign or add elements, no white shadow/halo, no opaque background. Output a neatly trimmed horizontal transparent PNG with modest transparent padding around the existing artwork, suitable for website header.

Logo secundário:

> Background extraction only of existing attached Maximos Confeitaria secondary logo. Remove all beige background to actual transparent alpha, including interiors of the two overlapping strawberry outlines. Preserve the exact two strawberry shapes, leaves, seeds, line thickness, terracotta colors and CONFEITARIA text with thin rules at each side. Do not add or redesign anything. No shadow, no white halo. Neatly trimmed transparent PNG with modest padding, suitable for website footer and favicon.
